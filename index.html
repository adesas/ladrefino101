// 01 · about

Who is this guy?

I'm Alex — Alejandro Benitez. I'm an electrical engineer in training (EIT) who works on the interesting intersection of power electronics, embedded systems, and things with wheels. By day it's circuit boards, CAN buses, and battery math; by evening it's soldering stations, 2 m antennas, and whatever project is currently upside down in the garage.

This site is a continuation of my old personal page from ~20 years ago — same person, newer decade. The music I made as a kid lives here too, under its own roof. More on that when you scroll to the bottom.

// 02 · engineering

Electrical & automotive EE

Embedded C, implementing custom CAN interfaces, power electronics — and enough multimeter hours to be dangerous. Current featured build:

Featured project · in progress

Steer-to-0 on a 2018 Kia Niro PHEV (Smart MDPS)

Comma 4 can read the steering CAN, but it's deliberately isolated from writing to it — safety first. The goal is low-speed autonomous steering using the car's own Smart MDPS system: implementing steer-to-zero with a standalone Panda running sunnypilot_smdps firmware and a custom harness into the EPS bus.
